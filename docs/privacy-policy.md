# Privacy Policy

**Cyprus Printmakers Association**
Effective date: 5 November 2026

## Who we are

Cyprus Printmakers Association ("the association", "we", "us") is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. We are a non-profit association based in Nicosia, Cyprus.

Our postal address is Aigeos 7, Palouriotissa 1035, Nicosia, Cyprus. You can reach us at cyprintmakers@gmail.com or +357 99351153.

This policy explains what personal data we collect through cyprintmakers.com and our related pages, why we collect it, who receives it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## What we collect

**When you contact us.** If you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message. If you email or call us, we receive whatever you choose to include.

**When you apply for membership.** The application form asks for your full name, email address, mobile number, a short explanation of why you want to join, and a CV. The CV is sent to the committee by email. It is not stored on the public website.

**Member profiles.** With a member's agreement, we publish their name, discipline, biography, portrait, website and Instagram handle on the members pages. Where a member has agreed to it, we also publish their email address so that others can contact them. A member can ask us to remove or change any of this at any time.

**Technical information.** Our hosting provider records standard request information such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. This is used to keep the site secure and working. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

**Your theme choice.** If you switch between the light and dark versions of the site, your browser stores that preference locally. It is not sent to us.

We do not create visitor accounts, take payments or process special category data through the site.

## Why we use it, and our legal bases

| What we do | Data involved | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Reply to enquiries sent through the contact form or by email | Name, email address, message and any details you include | Legitimate interests: responding to people who contact the association |
| Assess membership applications and administer membership | Name, email address, mobile number, motivation and CV | Legitimate interests: deciding on and managing membership. Where the law requires consent, we rely on the consent you give when you submit the form |
| Publish member profiles and member contact details | Name, discipline, biography, portrait, website, Instagram and, if agreed, email address | Consent |
| Keep the website secure and working | Technical request information | Legitimate interests: protecting our systems and visitors |
| Meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations | Information relevant to the obligation | Compliance with law |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have considered whether those interests are overridden by your rights. You can object to that processing at any time.

## Who receives it

We share personal data only where necessary:

- **Vercel** hosts the website and processes technical request information.
- **Sanity** stores the website content, including published member profiles.
- **Resend** delivers contact and membership emails, including CV attachments, to the committee inbox.
- **Committee members** who need the information to reply to you or decide on an application.
- **Authorities or advisers** where the law requires it, or where we need to establish or defend a legal claim.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

Some of our providers are based outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You can ask us for more information about the safeguard that applies.

## How long we keep it

- **Contact enquiries:** up to 12 months after the conversation ends.
- **Membership applications:** up to 12 months after a decision if the application is unsuccessful. For accepted members, we keep the application while the membership continues and for 12 months afterwards.
- **CVs:** for the same period as the application, then deleted.
- **Published member profiles:** until the member leaves or asks us to remove them.
- **Technical logs:** for the short period set by our hosting provider for security and operation.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it or remove anything that identifies you. If data is held in a backup, we keep it isolated until the backup is replaced.

## Security

We use HTTPS across the site, restrict editing access to committee members with accounts, and keep the website software and its dependencies up to date. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect the data we hold.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

- give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
- correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
- delete your data where we no longer have a good reason to keep it;
- restrict how we use it while a question about it is resolved;
- transfer a machine-readable copy to you or to another organisation;
- object to processing that relies on our legitimate interests;
- withdraw consent where we rely on consent, without affecting what we did before you withdrew it.

To make a request, email cyprintmakers@gmail.com or write to us at the address above. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We will answer within one month, and we will tell you if we need longer.

If you are not satisfied with our response or with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection, 1 Iasonos Street, 1082 Nicosia, Cyprus. Email commissioner@dataprotection.gov.cy or call +357 22818456. You can also complain to the supervisory authority in the country where you live.

## Cookies and local storage

We do not use cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking. The only preference we store in your browser is your choice of light or dark theme. You can clear it through your browser settings.

The content management interface at /admin is used by committee editors and uses authentication cookies. Visitors to the public site do not sign in.

## Children

The site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Automated decisions

We do not make automated decisions about you and we do not build profiles for marketing.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will tell you by email or with a notice on the site before it takes effect.
